OVERWATCH @VERSION@ - BUILDING ONE UNIT
===================================

Do the three folders in order. That is the whole procedure.

  1-print-the-case     two printed parts. Pick ONE of the two designs.
  2-flash-the-board    one command. Puts the software on the board.
  3-hand-it-over       what your friend runs on their own computer.

START-HERE.pdf is the same thing with pictures and detail. Print it and
keep it on the bench.

Overwatch-Manual.pdf is everything else: how it works, troubleshooting,
what every command does. You do not need it to build a unit.


THE ONE MISTAKE WORTH AVOIDING
------------------------------

Use the .bin in 2-flash-the-board. Do NOT download "overwatch-fw.bin"
from the releases page and flash that.

They look interchangeable and they are not. The one here contains the
bootloader AND the program. The one on the releases page is the program
only - it is meant for boards that already work, updating themselves.

Flash the wrong one and the board stays dark with no error. It looks
exactly like a bad solder joint, and you will spend an evening on it.


CHECKING YOUR WORK
------------------

When the unit is finished and plugged into your friend's computer:

    overwatch status

The App line and the Board line must show the SAME version - @VERSION@ and
@VERSION@. They ship together, so if they differ, one half is stale.
